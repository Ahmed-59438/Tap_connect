# TapConnect Brand Guidelines

## Brand Voice
- **Professional**: We handle identity and connections with care.
- **Futuristic**: We use the latest tech (NFC/BLE/AI) to solve old problems.
- **Minimal**: We value the user's time. Fewer clicks, more connections.

## Logo Usage
- **Primary Logo**: A stylized "T" and "C" interlocking, resembling two devices touching.
- **Color**: Electric Indigo gradient.
- **Clear Space**: Maintain a 16dp clear space around the logo.

## Iconography
- Use **Lucide** or **Google Symbols**.
- Style: **Outline**, 2dp stroke width.
- Colors: Primary for active icons, On-Surface for inactive.

## Interaction Design
- **Haptics**: Use subtle haptic pulses for success (NFC detection).
- **Animations**: Use spring animations for card transitions. No stiff linear movements.
- **Visual Feedback**: Pulsing rings around the profile photo when in "Networking Mode".

## Privacy Manifest
- Users must always see exactly what they are sharing.
- "Selective Sharing" is a brand pillar. Users are never forced to share their full profile.
